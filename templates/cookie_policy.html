Essential session cookies

This page explains the cookie-related behavior identified in the current Somo LeadFinder application. A cookie is a small data value stored by a website in your browser.

Somo LeadFinder uses Flask browser sessions to maintain sign-in and application state. The session cookie is configured as HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax; Secure is enabled when the application is configured for the Vercel runtime. These cookies support essential account and security functionality.

Google authentication

When you sign in with Google, your browser is sent to Google’s authentication service and later returned to Somo LeadFinder. Google may use cookies on its own pages and domains according to Google’s settings and policies. Somo LeadFinder does not control those third-party cookies.

Security and functionality

Session state and related browser functionality support authentication, request handling, and security controls. Search results are also held temporarily in the active page’s memory; that is not a browser cookie.

Analytics and advertising

The application source reviewed for this policy does not show an analytics or advertising cookie integration. This statement may need updating if additional analytics, advertising, embedded content, or third-party scripts are added.

Managing cookies

You can review, block, or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking essential session cookies may prevent sign-in or other account-dependent features from working correctly. Consult your browser’s help pages for its cookie controls.
