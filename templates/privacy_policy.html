1. Introduction

TYBWORK.LTD ("we", "our", or "us") operates Somo Leads, a business lead generation platform that helps users discover business information through Google Places services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you access or use our website and services.

By using Somo Leads, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

♟

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name received through Google Login, email address, Google account identifier, and profile picture, where provided and stored.
  • Account and Subscription Information: Current subscription plan, subscription status and expiration date, payment provider references, and usage and search quota information.
  • Search Information: Selected country and city, search keywords, search result counts, and Google Places identifiers associated with search results.
⚙

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users through Google Login.
  • Provide business search functionality and maintain search history.
  • Monitor usage and subscription limits and manage subscription access.
  • Provide customer support and maintain and improve our services.
◎

4. Google Login and Authentication

Somo Leads uses Google OAuth for user authentication.

When you sign in using Google, we may receive your name, email address, Google account identifier, and profile information according to the permissions granted.

Logging out only ends your active session. It does not automatically delete your account or associated information. If you revoke Google access or delete your Google account, your existing Somo Leads database record will not automatically be deleted.

◉

5. Cookies and Sessions

We use session cookies to support authentication and essential website functionality. Our application uses available cookie security settings, including HttpOnly, SameSite, and Secure settings where configured.

These cookies help maintain your login session and protect access to your account.

↗

6. Third-Party Services

Somo Leads uses third-party services to provide its functionality:

  • Google OAuth: Used to authenticate users and receive permitted Google profile information.
  • Google Places API: Used to search for business information based on user-selected locations and keywords.
  • Freemius: Used for subscription checkout, payment processing, billing-related services, and subscription management.

Payment processing is handled by Freemius. Somo Leads does not intentionally store full payment card numbers or CVV security codes in its application database.

These third-party providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

◷

7. Data Retention

We retain user information according to the following principles:

  • Account information, including name and email, remains stored while the user's Somo Leads account is maintained.
  • Logging out does not delete account information, and revoking Google access does not automatically delete existing database records.
  • Search history is retained across monthly subscription renewals and is not automatically reset each month.
  • Subscription information remains associated with the user's account.
  • Payment-related records are handled through Freemius and may be retained according to its applicable policies and legal requirements.

We do not currently specify a universal fixed retention period for all categories of information. Retention may be affected by applicable legal obligations and legitimate operational requirements.

$

8. Subscriptions and Cancellations

Somo Leads uses Freemius to manage paid subscriptions.

Logging out or logging back into your account does not cancel your subscription.

If you cancel a recurring subscription, your paid access generally remains available until the end of the applicable paid billing period, subject to the subscription terms and Freemius processing.

After the applicable expiration date, your account may move to the Free plan and its associated usage limits.

✓

9. Data Security

We use application-level security measures intended to protect user information, including authentication controls, session security settings, and webhook signature verification where implemented.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

♙

10. User Rights and Account Information

Users can view their name and email address through the Profile page. Users cannot directly edit these details through the application.

If you believe your account information is inaccurate, you may contact us using your registered email address. We may verify your identity before processing a correction request.

We aim to review and respond to correction requests within 3 business days. Where appropriate and technically possible, we will correct inaccurate information. If the information originates from Google, you may need to update your Google account information.

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning your personal information, please contact our support team.

♡

11. Children's Privacy

Somo Leads is a business-oriented service and is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
