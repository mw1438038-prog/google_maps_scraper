Information we receive

This policy describes how [BUSINESS / LEGAL NAME] (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information through Somo LeadFinder. Replace the bracketed business details and contact information before publication.

If you sign in with Google, Google OAuth supplies account identifiers and profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture when provided. The application stores account information needed to identify your user account, such as Google identity, email, name, and profile image.

Search history can include the keyword, city, country, time, result count, and export activity. For new searches, the application retains source labels and Google Places Place IDs where available. Full provider result details are returned for the active browser session and are not saved in the application's new search-history records. My Leads stores Google Place IDs and the related search metadata so that Google details can be requested when you choose to view them. Current CSV and Excel exports contain provider source labels, Google Place IDs, and the search query rather than full provider details.

Older records created before these data-handling changes may contain additional lead details. Those records are not automatically rewritten or deleted by this update.

Cookies and sessions

The application uses a browser session cookie to maintain sign-in and service state. Your browser also temporarily holds search results in memory for the active page, including while you move between result pages. Reloading or closing that page clears that in-memory result set.

Map and account providers

Google OAuth and Google Places are used for sign-in and Google Maps search when configured. Searches send the keyword and selected location to Google Places. Google Places content is displayed with Google attribution, and this application stores Google Place IDs rather than retaining full Google Places content for new searches.

Freemius handles subscription checkout and payment processing. The application receives subscription and transaction-related webhook information needed to manage plan status; payment card details are handled by the payment provider. The application may store provider identifiers, plan, subscription status, and expiry/usage information needed to administer account access; it does not need to store payment card details.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Account, usage, search-history, export metadata, subscription, and operational records are stored in the application's configured database. Search terms and locations are sent to Google Places to perform searches. The application does not sell personal information. Providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Search history and saved Place IDs do not currently have an automatic deletion schedule. Monitoring records have a 90-day retention target and are subject to the application's cleanup process. Export files downloaded to your device are outside the application's storage.

Security

The application uses authenticated sessions and access checks for account features. No method of internet transmission or storage can be described as completely secure; users should protect their account and downloaded files.
