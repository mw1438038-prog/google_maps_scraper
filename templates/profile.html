How do I find businesses?

Open Find Leads, choose a country and city, enter a business keyword, then run the search.

Where are my saved results?

Successful searches appear in My Leads and Search History.

How do I download leads?

Open Export Center and choose CSV or Excel beside a saved search.

Where can I see my search allowance?

Usage & Statistics shows searches used, searches remaining, and weekly activity.

How do I change my plan?
